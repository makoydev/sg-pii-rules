# Controls matrix (v0.1.0)

What each part of sg-pii-rules supports, with source and section. **P** = primary source read on 2026-09-30; **S** = secondary. The rules support the controls in [Vetted's matrix](https://github.com/makoydev/vetted/blob/next/CONTROLS.md) and, from Milestone 2, Discreet's.

## Gaps

- **Names, street addresses, dates of birth:** not detected yet. Unit numbers and dates of birth are planned for Milestone 2; card numbers and postal codes were added after v0.1.0 (rows 4b, 4c). Names need NER (a Presidio sidecar in Discreet, later).
- **No labelled real-world recall measurement:** that's impossible without real personal data. Discreet's Milestone 2 benchmark generates about 5,000 varied samples independently of these patterns.

Source links are listed in [Vetted's CONTROLS.md](https://github.com/makoydev/vetted/blob/next/CONTROLS.md#sources) and, for the NRIC algorithm, in [VALIDATORS.md](VALIDATORS.md).
